# Crayons Bridge: Strategic Architecture & Roadmap

## Vision
Crayons Bridge is not just a MAM (Media Asset Management) system; it is the **Operational Layer** for the global Indian film supply chain. It orchestrates the lifecycle from production completion to global OTT/AI training distribution.

## Core Architectural Pillars
1. **Business Workflow over Storage:** Orchestrate relationships and rights, not just files.
2. **Content Supply Chain:** Manage the acquisition, verification (QC), and delivery pipeline.
3. **Regional Focus, Global Reach:** Specialized for Indic languages (Malayalam, Tamil, etc.) with Big Tech/OTT standards.

## The Orchestration Stack (Implementation Priority)
1. **Production ERP Integration:** Onboarding film projects and production metadata.
2. **Title Registry & Metadata Manager:** Standardized schemas (EIDR/MEC) for regional assets.
3. **Rights & Licensing Manager:** Tracking territorial, platform, and language-specific rights.
4. **Partner & Buyer CRM:** Managing relationships with OTTs (JioStar, SonyLIV) and AI data buyers.
5. **Delivery Manager:** Automated delivery workflows (DPP/EML specs).
6. **Revenue & Contract Tracking:** Licensing marketplace and contract management.
