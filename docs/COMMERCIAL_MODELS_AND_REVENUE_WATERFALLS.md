# StreamVista Commercial Models and Revenue Waterfalls

## Default commercial policy

Default StreamVista / Crayons Pictures commission: **35%**.
Default rights-holder share: **65%**.

This is a default, not an automatic entitlement. The signed deal controls the final commercial basis, deductions, recoupment, participant shares, territory, language, term, rights and reporting obligations.

Canonical calculation for a standard commission deal:

`Verified Gross Revenue -> Contract-Approved Deductions -> Net Receipts -> Contractual Recoupment -> Net Distributable Revenue -> 35% StreamVista / Crayons -> 65% Rights Holder`

## Commercial pricing models

### Fixed licence fee
A fixed amount for a defined rights package, territory, language, exclusivity and term.

### Minimum Guarantee (MG)
A guaranteed minimum consideration. The contract must say whether the MG is recoupable, non-recoupable or creditable against later revenue share.

### MG + revenue share
An MG is paid first, followed by an agreed share of revenue after the defined recoupment or threshold rules.

### Revenue share
Verified revenue is split according to the contract. StreamVista's default commercial position is 35% unless a specific deal overrides it.

### Outright assignment
A sale or assignment of specifically identified rights. "Outright" must never be treated as automatically meaning all copyright, all territories, all languages and perpetual term. The agreement must define exactly what is assigned.

### Distribution commission
StreamVista / Crayons distributes authorised content and earns a commission from the agreed calculation base.

### Syndication commission
StreamVista / Crayons packages or places authorised rights with downstream buyers and earns the agreed syndication commission.

### Transaction commission
A percentage or fee per completed transaction, rental, purchase or other monetised event.

### Service fee
A fixed or variable fee for services such as QC, localisation, delivery, rights administration, packaging or reporting, separate from content revenue.

### Subscription plan
A monthly or annual software / platform fee. It may coexist with transaction or licensing commissions where the contract permits.

### Acquisition fee
A fixed or percentage fee for sourcing, negotiating or closing an acquisition.

### Cost-plus
Approved costs plus an agreed service margin.

### Hybrid
Any approved combination of fixed fee, MG, revenue share, commission, subscription, transaction fee and service fee.

## Payment bases

A contract may calculate commission or revenue share on one of several bases:

- Licence fee
- Minimum Guarantee
- Gross billed
- Gross collected
- Net receipts
- Net distributable revenue
- Advertising revenue
- Transaction revenue
- Subscription allocation
- Package allocation
- Flat service fee
- Custom contract-defined base

The basis must be explicit. A percentage without a defined calculation base is incomplete.

## Approved deductions

Deductions are allowed only when the contract permits them and evidence exists. Examples include taxes, withholding, GST, refunds, chargebacks, platform fees, payment-gateway fees, sales-agent fees, localisation costs, delivery costs, marketing costs, approved third-party costs and contractual recoupment.

## Reporting and evidence sources

Preferred order:

`Platform API -> Platform Export -> CSV/XLSX/SFTP -> Email/PDF Statement -> Payment Gateway / Bank Evidence -> Verified Manual Entry -> Authorised Public Collection`

Revenue must not be inferred from views alone unless the contract itself defines a verified per-view payment formula.

## Model examples

**SVOD fixed licence**
`Licence Fee -> Approved Taxes/Deductions -> Commission Base -> 35% StreamVista / Crayons -> 65% Rights Holder`

**AVOD / FAST revenue share**
`Verified Ad Revenue -> Approved Platform Deductions -> Net Revenue -> Recoupment if contracted -> 35/65 or deal-specific split`

**TVOD / EST**
`Transactions -> Refunds/Chargebacks -> Platform Fees -> Net Transaction Revenue -> Agreed Split`

**MG + revenue share**
`MG -> Contractual Recoupment Ledger -> Additional Revenue -> Agreed Post-Recoupment Split`

**Outright**
`Agreed Assignment Consideration -> Approved Deductions if any -> Contractual Commission -> Rights-Holder Settlement`

**Distribution / syndication**
`Collected Revenue -> Approved Costs -> Distribution/Syndication Commission -> Remaining Participant Shares`

## ROI

ROI is a reporting metric, not a replacement for settlement accounting.

`ROI % = (Return - Investment) / Investment x 100`

Possible views include creator ROI, StreamVista project ROI and buyer ROI where verified cost and return evidence is available.

## Settlement rule

No deal may be marked paid or settled from analytics alone. Settlement requires authoritative commercial evidence such as the executed agreement, platform statement, invoice, payment receipt, tax evidence, approved waterfall and human finance verification.
