**Convert existing StreamVista + Crayons Pictures capabilities into the first verified customer payments within 7 days.**

> **Cash collected — not features shipped.**

# 8. DAY 5 — OUTBOUND

Do not wait for organic traffic.

Start direct outreach.

### Daily target

**30–50 qualified conversations**

with personalized outreach.

Message structure:

```text
I’m building a service through StreamVista for filmmakers
who already have completed/near-completed projects but need
the content packaged properly for OTT/buyer conversations.

We can audit the project, identify rights/QC/metadata gaps
and prepare a buyer-ready package.

If you have a film currently looking for OTT/distribution,
I can review the project and tell you what is missing.
```

The objective is **conversation → assessment → paid package**.

---

# 9. DAY 6 — CONVERT

Founder personally handles the first sales conversations.

Do not automate high-value sales too early.

For every prospect:

### Ask

1. What is the project?
2. Is it completed?
3. Who owns the rights?
4. What territories/languages are available?
5. Has it been submitted to OTT/buyers?
6. What happened?
7. What is the immediate commercial objective?

Then prescribe the correct package.

## Sprint Definition of Done

The first cashflow sprint is complete only when:

**At least one real customer has paid real money,**

**the payment is verified in the authoritative payment ledger,**

**the customer has entered a real fulfillment workflow,**

**the promised service has been delivered or is actively being fulfilled,**

and

**the resulting sales/conversion evidence has been captured for the next sprint.**
