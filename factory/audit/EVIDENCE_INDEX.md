## Evidence requirements
Every entry must include:
- component/product
- repository
- branch/ref/commit
- action performed
- verification method
- result
- timestamp
- blocker or rollback information when applicable

## Rule
Audit is evidence produced by execution. Do not create repeat audit loops without a concrete change, incident, release, or verification target. Do not convert policy, design, repository-name similarity or an unverified URL into implementation/deployment proof.
